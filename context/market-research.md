# Market Research: ANZ

Researched 2026-10-27 from web search. **Source quality is mostly vendor and
consultancy blogs (tag S = secondary).** Nothing here has been checked against
primary sources (tag P) such as AASB, Treasury, XRB, FMA, MfE. Verify every
figure against a primary source before it appears in the product, a report or a
sales document. Section 6 lists what to check.

## 1. Why now

Large ANZ companies are being pushed to collect structured data from their
supply chains. The pressure comes from climate disclosure, packaging regulation
and procurement policy. Suppliers are mostly SMEs without tooling, and buyers
are chasing them by spreadsheet and email. Circlo's wedge is a standard
circularity questionnaire, free and login-free for suppliers.

### What this means for Circlo
- The strongest, most certain demand is Scope 3 / value-chain data pressure on
  large entities from 2027 onward, but **Circlo does not produce emissions
  data**. It produces structured circularity evidence that sits beside it.
- The Treasury proposal to set boundaries on supplier information requests
  cuts both ways: it may reduce ad hoc requests, but it favours **one
  proportionate, standard questionnaire** over many bespoke ones. Treat that as
  a design principle (short, clear, reusable), not a guaranteed demand driver.
- Packaging and product stewardship are the sharper circularity drivers for
  retail/FMCG and agriculture, and the rules are still moving. Expect buyers
  to ask "what will this look like when the scheme lands?". Questionnaire v1
  should map to packaging design and recycled-content questions without
  claiming to meet any scheme.

## 3. Segments and verticals

Primary buyer: large corporates (AASB S2 Group 1–2 equivalents, NZ climate
reporting entities, and their suppliers' customers). Government buyers are
secondary.

| Vertical | Circularity pain | Questionnaire emphasis (hypothesis) | Proof needed |
| -------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Retail and FMCG / packaging | Packaging design obligations, recycled content, own-brand supplier base | Materials and packaging, product design, take-back | Interviews with a packaging or sustainability lead |
| Construction and infrastructure | Large subcontractor and materials supplier base, waste-heavy, tender-driven | Materials, waste and by-products, reuse of materials | Interviews with a procurement or sustainability lead at a tier-1 contractor |
| Logistics and supply chain | Packaging, pallets, fleet, warehouse waste; fits Fliway/SCS relationships | Waste, energy and water, packaging | Use internal contacts for first pilot (see section 5) |
| Agriculture and food | Farm plastics, packaging, export-market traceability demands | Materials and packaging, waste, governance | Confirm farm-plastics scheme status first |

These are hypotheses. None has been validated with a customer.

Gap to test, not to claim: no ANZ-native, circularity-specific supplier
assessment platform surfaced. Search was shallow; run a proper competitor scan
(ANZ consultancies, industry bodies, NZ Product Stewardship, APCO tools,
the Supply Chain Sustainability School) before saying this externally.

### Differentiation hypotheses to test
1. Free, login-free for suppliers (versus supplier-paid ratings).
2. Circularity-specific questionnaire and bands, tuned to ANZ packaging and
   waste context.
3. Fast onboarding: import suppliers by CSV, first campaign in a day.
4. ANZ data residency and local support.

## 5. Go-to-market notes

- Beachhead: logistics / supply chain via existing Fliway, SCS and NZ Post
  relationships, then retail and packaging. Check relationship, conflict and
  permission questions in `progress-tracker.md` before using group contacts.
- Demo with synthetic data only (no real supplier PII).
- Pricing: no validated numbers. Large-corporate procurement usually buys annual
  contracts by supplier count or suppliers-assessed. Test willingness to pay in
  discovery calls. The $99 / $299 SME tiers inherited from the sibling project
  are not a reference.
- Procurement hurdles to prepare for: security questionnaire, data residency,
  privacy impact assessment, SSO request, and an SLA. Several are "Later" in
  `project-overview.md`; expect them to surface in sales conversations.

## 6. Verification checklist (do before external use)

- [ ] AASB S2 thresholds, start dates and Scope 3 relief against the AASB
      standard and Treasury / ASIC guidance.
- [ ] Whether Group 3 gets Scope 3 deferral; assurance timetable.
- [ ] Outcome of the Treasury consultation and the Budget threshold change.
- [ ] Current status of Australian packaging legislation and APCO obligations
      (DCCEEW).
- [ ] NZ CS 2 adoption relief periods, number of climate reporting entities, and
      any NZ IFRS S2 transition decision (XRB, FMA).
- [ ] NZ plastic packaging stewardship scheme status, farm-plastics scheme date
      (MfE).
- [ ] Current NZ Government Procurement Rules text and any circular-economy
      guidance (Procurement NZ).
- [ ] Competitor pricing and ANZ presence, from vendor or customer sources.
- [ ] Customer discovery: at least five buyer interviews across the four
      verticals, recording who owns supplier data collection today.

## 7. Customer discovery questions

1. Who in your organisation requests sustainability data from suppliers today,
   and how (spreadsheet, portal, email)?
2. How many suppliers, and how many are SMEs?
3. Which regulation or customer demand triggers the request?
4. What do you do with the answers (disclosure, tender scoring, risk)?
5. What do you pay for tools today, and who signs?
6. What would make you trust a score from a new vendor?
7. What are your hosting, security and privacy requirements?
